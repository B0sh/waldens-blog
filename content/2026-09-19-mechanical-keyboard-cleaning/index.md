A nice [gaming keyboard from Corsair](https://www.corsair.com/us/en/p/keyboards/ch-9000088-na/strafe-mechanical-gaming-keyboard-cherry-mx-red-ch-9000088-na) caught my eye at Goodwill this week. And for $5.99, I thought I'd take a chance on it. Thankfully, the keys all worked, but the condition was really rough. It looks heavily used (Great!), but I wanted to see if I could give this product a second life with a good cleaning. It turns out cleaning keyboards isn't too hard, if only a little tedious.

### Step #1: Remove the keys

I just used my hands for this, but some keyboards may require a [key puller](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Keycap_pullers). Look away if allergic to gunk.

### Step #2: Wash with dish soap

I ran the keys over some water for a bit to get the larger food items out, and then let them soak in warm water with a bit of dish soap for 45 minutes. 



{{< slideshow
  id="keyboard-washing"

### Step #3: Clean the board

I tried 3 things: microfiber cloth, compressed air, and a Q-Tip. 

The cloth was nice to get most of the debris unlodged, but it couldn't get in the tiny crevices of the keyboard. So I tried compressed air, which was better still, but again not satisfying enough. Only after taking several Q-Tips through literally every inch of the board did I end up being satisfied with my result. Also I used that microfiber cloth to wipe down the casing, which only was a little dirty.

Also: I realized after I finished perhaps I should've considered a full disassembly of the keyboard.

### Step #4: Dry the key caps

I drained the key caps out, wiped them down individually, and lined everything up. I went pretty quick with the drying since these will be left out overnight to dry.

It was also here that I discovered that I lost my `6` key, which took me just as long to find as the last step as I kicked it across the room under my oven without realizing...

### Step #5: Rebuild and enjoy

After waiting for the next day, all that's left is to reattach the keys!

I wanted to be more thorough with the board cleaning, but this is a night and day improvement. Super happy with my result here!
